# Game rules

One to six players begin with $50 each. All money is fictional. The minimum
stake is $2, with a maximum of $1,000 or the player's bankroll. There are six
runners per race, with quoted integer odds from 2:1 to 20:1.

Win requires first place and returns stake multiplied by odds. Place requires
top two and returns stake plus 20–25% of stake multiplied by (odds minus one).
Show requires top three and returns stake plus 10–15% of stake multiplied by
(odds minus one). The Place/Show percentages are drawn once per race and shared.

Original stops once three runners finish. Remastered continues until all
surviving runners finish. Fallen jockeys are out; four falls void a race and
refund stakes. A visit ends when all players have less than $2.

Remastered also offers Quinella (top two in either order), Exacta (ordered top
two), Trifecta (ordered top three) and Box Trifecta (top three in any order).
Their total returns are stake times the product of selected odds, divided by
2 for Quinella and by 6 for Box Trifecta. These are fixed game quotes, not
real-world pool dividends. The stake is the total ticket cost.

See [Remastered design](form-mode.md) for persistent traits, hidden daily form,
recent history and weighted race movement.
