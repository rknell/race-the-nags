A recreation of the 1992 horse racing classic, with original rules, a new Remastered mode, and room for six players at the same screen. All bets use play money.

No account needed. Best on a desktop or laptop.

Choose your era

Original racing rules or Remastered form, stamina and consistency. Classic 1992 sprites, new illustrated artwork or animated horses.

Back your judgement

Win, Place and Show, plus Quinellas, Exactas and Trifectas in Remastered. Race by race or let an automatic strategy run.

Bring your friends

One to six players sharing a screen. Racing records, remembered names and save/continue between visits.

Browser progress stays in this browser on this website. Clearing site data can remove saves. Desktop downloads keep separate saves. Click or tap the game to enable sound.
