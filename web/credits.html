A tribute to Race the Nags

Original game and Classic graphics: Richard “Dick” Olsen / Olsen Outdoors, 1992. We offer this independent recreation free of charge, without advertising or paid access, to help keep a classic game alive. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

Classic graphics preserve the original portrait and 30 horse/rider images. The Illustrated and Animated artwork, code and new horse-name roster are newly authored. The original DOS programs are not distributed.
