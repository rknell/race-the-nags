# Attribution, preservation and takedown notice

## A free tribute to Race the Nags

Race the Nags (1992) was created by **Richard “Dick” Olsen / Olsen Outdoors**.
This independent, non-commercial tribute aims to preserve the character of the
original game and introduce it to new players. It is offered free of charge,
without advertising, paid access or sales of the original artwork. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official release of Olsen Outdoors or any
rightsholder, heir or estate.

Historical reference: https://archive.org/details/RaceTheNags

## Original graphics and rights

The **Classic — 1992 graphics** option uses the original title portrait and
30 horse/rider images decoded from the supplied NAG.PCX and PONY.IMG files.
The selected images are in `godot/assets/heritage/`; `provenance.json` identifies
their source and SHA-256 hashes. The original DOS programs, raw PCX/IMG files,
manuals, extracted horse-name roster and decompiled reference evidence are not
included in the public repository or releases.

Original graphics remain the property of their respective copyright holders.
**They are not covered by this project's MIT licence.** We claim no ownership
of those images and grant no licence to reuse them. The supplied shareware
terms concern distribution of the original evaluation package and do not
explicitly authorize their reuse in this reconstruction. Additional permission
for that reuse has not been documented. Free availability, attribution and this
takedown offer are not a substitute for permission and are not a claim that the
images are public domain, royalty-free, or cleared for redistribution.

## Rightsholders, heirs and estate representatives

If you own or represent rights in the original game or graphics, including as
a rightful heir or estate representative, please contact the maintainer via:

https://github.com/rknell/race-the-nags/issues/new?title=Rights%20or%20takedown%20request

Identify the material concerned and your relationship to the rights. Please do
not post private identity documents publicly; an initial issue can be used to
arrange further contact. On receiving a request from a rightful owner or
authorized representative, we will promptly remove the affected material from
the hosted game, maintained public repository and release downloads, or take
the project down if requested. We welcome corrections to credits and provenance.
We cannot recall copies already downloaded or held in third-party caches.
